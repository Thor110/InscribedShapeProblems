Dimension Explorer

The skeletal jig in any dimension: rays from a pivot along the vertex directions of a regular polytope, solved until every ray has the same length. Each cell runs a fresh search and reports whether an inscribed copy was verified (all pairwise distances and surface distances to 1e-9) or the closest miss.
Press Run to fill the table
Expected dimension = dimension of similarities of ℝⁿ, n(n+1)/2 + 1, minus the number of vertices. When it is negative (amber), a generic surface has no inscribed copy; this is proved in PROOFS.md (Theorem 1), so those cells are expected to fail except on symmetric surfaces such as the sphere and ellipsoid. Hypercube rows in 5D and above have 32+ vertices and take longest.

Verified Results

Every inscribed shape recorded here was rebuilt from its curve or surface seed and verified independently (all pairwise distances and every vertex's distance from the curve or surface, to a relative error of 1e-9). Only the first result for each problem is recorded. When a finder verifies a shape that is not listed, it offers to submit it; an automated check opens a pull request.

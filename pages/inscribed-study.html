Inscribed Shape Study

The open part of the square problem is rough, merely continuous curves: could inscribed squares shrink to a point as a curve gets rougher? This page follows squares through ever-rougher fractal curves, and rectangles across aspect ratios (the rectangular peg problem). Every square and rectangle counted here passed the 1e-9 verification. Click any point to see its curve and largest shape.

Tetrahedron shrinkage (3D)

Regular tetrahedra on a smooth surface come in a 3-parameter family, one for every orientation. Sixteen of them, at fixed starting orientations, are carried through every level of roughness: each new octave of detail is blended in gradually while each tetrahedron's position and orientation adjust. The chart shows the smallest one still being carried.
